## What would flip a no

- A trade or designer discount (15 to 20% off), or a $10 to $15 physical sample or swatch of the paper and fixture finish shipped to me before I commit to the full price.
- A pile of verified reviews from real buyers, or the same fixture listed on Amazon or Wayfair where I already trust the returns.
- A solid pile of independent reviews (Trustpilot, Google, photos from real buyers in real rooms) or seeing the store mentioned by a design blog or renovation forum I trust.
- Real customer photos of installed pieces, close-up video showing the material and finish, or a cheap fabric or material sample I could hold before ordering.
- A bunch of verified reviews with real customer photos of the pendant hanging in a home, or a cheap swatch/sample so I can see the material in person.
- A real discount or trade pricing that brings the price near $250, plus a handful of verified reviews with photos, or a cheap sample or swatch to check the quality first.
- A trade or multi-unit price clearly below what the lookalikes cost, plus a firm delivery date well inside 2 weeks, and a few verified reviews from other hosts or designers.
- A decent number of verified reviews with real customer photos, or the same pendant at a retailer I already trust.
- A solid pile of independent reviews (Trustpilot or similar) from real buyers with photos, and some history showing the store has been around and handles returns and damage claims properly.
- A guaranteed in-stock delivery date within 7 to 10 days, or a few in-stock pieces that ship in days.
- A firm ship-by date guaranteed to arrive before the electrician visit, plus a free sample or swatch of the paper shade, or a meaningful number of verified reviews with photos of installed fixtures.
- Photos from real buyers with the light hung in an actual kitchen, plus a confirmed delivery date that clears my electrician with a week to spare.
