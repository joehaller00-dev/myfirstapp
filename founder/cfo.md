## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $364 a month (Shopify Basic plan $39, Apps: Judge.me reviews, delivery-date, email (est.) $100, Google Workspace, domain, Google Voice (est.) $25, Ongoing QC samples and photography (est.) $200).

- **Break-even: 1 orders a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (1 a day): **5%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 20 a day.
