# Marketing: Nora Furnish

Evidence used: panel v1 (0/100 buy, trust 88), v2 (0/20 after the honest offer), v3 target state (9/20 buy), competitors.md, pricing.md, numbers.json, Shopify traffic data. **Rule for everything below: no ad money until store-fixes.md Week 1 and Week 2 are done.** Traffic onto today's store buys refunds and Merchant Center risk, not customers.

## 1. Positioning

1. *For design-minded homeowners finishing a room, who can't tell which online lighting stores are real, Nora Furnish is the lighting shop that shows you everything up front (where it ships from, when it arrives, the UL listing, free returns), unlike the look-alike stores that hide it.* **(recommended)** Evidence: the buyers who said yes in v3 named exactly these proofs ("a UL number I can look up and free 30-day returns answer..."). The competitor gap: no look-alike DTC store states origin and lead time up front (competitors.md §6).
2. *For people with a kitchen island or stairwell to light, Nora Furnish sizes it for you, free, and sells the set.* Evidence: sizing help and spec sheets reduced effort objections; the bundle ladder lifts order value.
3. *For Oklahoma designers and rental hosts, a local lighting source with trade pricing and project quotes.* Evidence: trade buyers bought in v3 because of the 15% rate; local showrooms (LifeStyles Edmond, Andrews Lighting Gallery OKC) are the comparison.

Use 1 for the site and Google; 3 as a parallel direct-outreach channel.

## 2. Channels (pick three)

| Channel | Why it fits | Cost | How you know it worked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google organic + free Shopping listings** | The only channel that has converted (1,889 sessions, 7 orders, 0.37%). 31 buyer-intent guides already exist. | $0 + time | Organic orders per month; guides → product clicks |
| **Google Shopping ads on 20-30 hero SKUs** (standard Shopping, not broad Performance Max) | Buyers searching "cloud pendant light", "staircase chandelier" have intent; ads show price and image up front, so the honest price rule matters | $30/day test = ~$900/month | CAC ≤ $100; contribution per click > 0 |
| **Local trade outreach (OKC/Edmond)** | 30 designers, builders and STR hosts; trust borrowed from a face and a phone call | Founder time + samples | 3 trade accounts with a first order in 30 days |
| Pinterest organic (support) | 15% of buyers are savers; the Pinterest tag is already installed | Time | Saves → site sessions |

**Not doing now:** Meta/Instagram ads (cold audiences and this store's trust gap; past Facebook traffic was 187 add-to-carts and 0 orders, bot-like), TikTok, influencers and Performance Max on the full catalog. Revisit after 30 real reviews.

## 3. The most you can pay for a customer

Contribution per order before acquisition = $350 − $87.50 − $30 − $10.45 − $35 − $17.50 − $20.30 = **$149.25** (numbers.json). The panel shows about 1 purchase per buyer in the first month, and repeat buying is unproven. So the **ceiling is ~$149 per first order; target ≤ $100** to keep ~$49 per order. At today's 0.15% conversion and ~$1 per Shopping click, CAC would be ~$670. **Conversion has to reach ~1% before ads pay.** That is why fixes come first.
