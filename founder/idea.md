# Nora Furnish

- What it is: a US online store (Shopify, norafurnish.com) selling modern and statement lighting plus home decor, positioned as high-ticket ("modern lighting and home decor with free US shipping and 30 day returns").
- Who it is for: US homeowners furnishing or renovating (pendants, chandeliers, sconces, outdoor lanterns), plus a stated trade channel: designers, contractors, hospitality, short-term-rental owners, offices and retail (pages for each exist). Target customer not yet validated with data.
- What it sells, at what price: 1,819 active products (about 1,187 lighting, the rest decor, soft furnishings, seasonal). Prices: 595 under $100, 854 at $100-300, 596 at $300-600, 398 at $600-1,000, 391 at $1,000+. Typical lighting item $200-$600. Unit costs recorded in Shopify run roughly 5x-17x below retail (e.g. a ceiling light costing $8.66 sells at $123.99).
- Where and how: online only, US only, free standard shipping, no minimum. "In stock" items arrive in 10-12 business days; made-to-order 3-4 weeks production + 9-10 business days transit. Fulfilled through suppliers (no inventory held: every active product shows 0 units with "continue selling"). 30-day returns, buyer pays return shipping on change-of-mind. Discounts: 10% first order, 10% on 2 items, 15% on 3+, paid membership with 20% off.
- Budget and constraints: solo founder (Edmond, Oklahoma), Shopify Basic plan. Marketing budget, cash available and time per week are NOT known yet (open question for /founder-cfo). Store has been live roughly since February 2026.
