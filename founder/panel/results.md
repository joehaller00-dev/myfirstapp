## What would flip a no

- A clearly shown UL/ETL listing on the product page, plus free returns (or at least a prepaid return label) and some proof the store is real, like reviews from verified buyers or a known retailer behind it.
- A lot of independent reviews (Trustpilot, Google, photos from real customers in real rooms), a free return on a change of mind or a sample of the material, and some sign the company has been around for years.
- Hundreds of verified reviews with customer photos on Trustpilot or similar, plus free returns and a faster, firm delivery date.
- Real customer reviews with photos of the actual product, a firm delivery date that beats my electrician date, and free return shipping or a cheap fabric or finish sample before I commit.
- Free returns, or a prepaid return label for anything damaged or not as pictured. Real customer photos and reviews of the pendant hanging in actual homes would help too.
- A trade or designer program with a free sample or swatch, free returns, real reviews with customer photos, and a clear spec sheet (materials, certifications like UL/ETL listing, dimensions). Faster guaranteed delivery would help too.
- Visible UL/ETL listing on the product page, plus a solid pile of independent reviews (Trustpilot or similar) from real buyers with photos, and a clear delivery date that lands before my electrician's visit.
- A solid base of independent reviews (Trustpilot or similar) with real customer photos, plus a way to see the material in person, like a cheap sample or a free-return policy.
- Free return shipping or a clear damage guarantee, real customer photos and reviews, and a faster delivery date. A sale or promo code beyond 10% off would also help.
- A full spec sheet with UL/ETL listing, lumens, CRI, wattage, dimensions and mounting details, plus real reviews with customer photos and a verifiable company address and phone number. Free return shipping on defects or on anything that arrives damaged would help too.
- A guaranteed delivery date within about 7 days on in-stock pieces, plus free returns, or reviews from real buyers with photos showing the quality.
- A pile of real, verifiable customer reviews (Trustpilot or similar) with photos of this light, plus free return shipping or a price clearly below Amazon for the same fixture.

## Moderator's read (added after tally)

**Headline: 0 buy, 100 pass.** Every segment, behaviour and income band scored 0%. This matches the live store, which converts about 0.1% of real sessions.

**Why they pass:** trust 88, timing 8, price 4. Trust is not about price. Buyers will not put $360 on an unknown store that shows no proof, no UL/ETL listing, no specs and no free returns.

**What would flip a no (keyword count across all 100 "would change my mind" answers):**

| Lever | Buyers who named it |
|---|---:|
| Free returns or a prepaid return label | 94 |
| Real reviews with customer photos (on-site plus Trustpilot/Google) | 91 |
| Faster or firm, dated delivery | 51 |
| UL/ETL listing and a full spec sheet (lumens, CRI, wattage, mounting) | 30 |
| A lower price than the same item elsewhere / a bigger discount | 28 |
| Samples or swatches / seeing it in person | 27 |
| Trade/designer program | 6 |
| Phone number, address, years in business | 5 |

**Price answers for the $359.99 pendant (medians):** too cheap $90, bargain $195, getting expensive $400, too expensive $650. These are for an unknown store; /founder-pricing turns them into a range.

**Caveat on the pitch:** the pitch told buyers this is a store they have not heard of. That is true for almost every new visitor, but it puts trust front of mind. Even read generously, the result says the same thing as the 0.1% real conversion rate: the offer has no proof. Simulated buyers lean agreeable, so 0% is a hard floor signal, not noise.

**What to change before spending a dollar on ads:** free returns on lighting, a reviews engine with photos, UL/ETL and spec sheets on every lighting product page, and a dated delivery promise. That is /founder-offer's job. More traffic will not fix it.
